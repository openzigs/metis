- A company-policy question may again use everyday words such as "business rules", "the pricing
  rules engine" or "a validation rule". Only a question that points at a particular rule, clause
  or policy ("the seven-year retention rule", "clause 4b", "under the data retention policy") is
  reworded. When the generic question is used, it still quotes your requirement even if the
  requirement itself talks about rules.
