- Clarification answers are filed under the requirement the question was about.
  When two neighbouring requirements fitted the question equally well, the
  answers went to whichever came first in the list. Now each requirement is
  matched to its own row first, and a tie is reported as "not applied" rather
  than guessed. An answer for a rejected requirement is no longer filed under
  the requirement next to it.
