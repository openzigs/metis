- The chat citation contract's effect on answers was re-measured over five paired BA question-set
  runs a side: no drop in facts stated was detected. Of the 11 questions chat answered, 7 held, 3
  fell by one fact in one run of three and 1 rose (the other 4 answerable questions are structural
  zeros: chat abstains on them in every run). The mean per-question difference was -0.4 points
  (after minus before, over all 15), and a drop of several points cannot be ruled out. Citation
  precision on cited lines held at 99%. Tool results cut to fit the model's context about doubled,
  with no sign that answers that hit a cut stated fewer facts, so the result headers and the
  default cap are unchanged.
