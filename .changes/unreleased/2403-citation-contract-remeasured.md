- The chat citation contract's effect on answers was re-measured over five paired BA question-set
  runs a side: facts stated did not drop (11 of 15 answerable questions scored the same with and
  without it; the mean per-question difference was 0.4 points), and citation precision on cited
  lines held at 99%. Tool results cut to fit the model's context about doubled, but answers that
  hit a cut stated no fewer facts, so the result headers and the default cap are unchanged.
