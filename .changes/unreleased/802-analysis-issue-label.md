- Every published issue now carries the umbrella `metis` label plus one source
  label: `metis-analysis` (Deep Dive, was `metis-scanner`), `metis-impact-analysis`
  (Impact Analysis to Jira, was `metis-scanner`) or `metis-scanner` (bug scanner).
  Suggested or added labels naming another source are dropped. Existing issues
  are not relabelled.
