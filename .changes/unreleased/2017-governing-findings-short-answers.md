section: Fixed

- A short answer from a business analyst ("Yes, we do.") can again support a
  Compliant governing finding, when the finding quotes it together with the
  question it answers; before, such answers were marked as a Gap. Findings
  refreshed after several answers now land in the order the answers were
  given, and the findings list says when a refresh is still running. When a
  rule stops applying to a requirement, the accept or reject decision on its
  finding is now recorded in the audit log, note included. Still behind the
  `GOVERNING_DOCS_ENABLED` setting, which is off by default.
