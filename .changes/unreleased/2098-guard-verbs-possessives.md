section: Fixed

- Company-policy questions such as "Does the app use pricing rules?" or "Does the system follow
  rules for refunds?" are no longer reworded as if they pointed at the rulebook, so they no longer
  cost a second rewording pass. A question that names a particular rule through a possessive ("the
  retention rule's limit", "the retention policy's terms") is reworded again, as it was before.
