- A change request, generated document or upload you started now ends with a toast and a bell
  item that name it and open the result (or where to retry a failure, or the quarantine queue).
  One toast and one bell item per job, also across tabs. Switch: Settings → Notifications →
  **Analysis, ingest and document results** (on by default).
- **Questions waiting (N)** on the step header, the change-request list and the Overview.
- A change request shows its review: **Review assigned to you**, **Your review request was
  rejected**, or its status, linked to the review.
