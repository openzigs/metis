section: Fixed

- A company-policy rule is no longer ruled out when your answer to its clarifying question
  contradicts the requirement. If the requirement sends customer data to an outside company
  and the answer says nothing leaves your systems, the rule still applies. Its finding is then
  checked again with your answer, instead of being removed. If the finding remains, you can
  correct the requirement or reject the finding with a note. An answer that the requirement
  really does not involve the rule's subject still rules it out.
