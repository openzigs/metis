- A company-policy finding no longer disappears when your answer to its clarifying question
  contradicts the requirement. If the requirement sends customer data to an outside company
  and the answer says nothing leaves your systems, the rule still applies, and the recorded
  reason says the answer contradicts the requirement. The finding stays on the Governing rules
  panel, where you can correct the requirement or reject the finding with a note. An answer
  that the requirement really does not involve the rule's subject still rules it out.
