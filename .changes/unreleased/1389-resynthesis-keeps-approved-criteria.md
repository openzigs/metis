issue: 1389
section: Fixed
---

- A Regenerate no longer changes an approved requirement's acceptance criteria. The criteria approved at the checkpoint are kept and the requirement stays Approved. A materially different re-synthesis is offered in one notice per analysis (Review, Accept, Dismiss, or keep approved criteria for all); an empty or merely reworded one is not. Clarification answers are written back into the re-created requirements.
