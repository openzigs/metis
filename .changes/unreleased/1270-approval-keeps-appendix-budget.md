- Reader BRD: approving, un-approving or editing a requirement, the first approval included, no longer rewrites the appendix's catalogue sections at model cost. The Approved Requirements section now sets aside the same room as the reconstructed requirements, however long the list is, so regenerating re-renders only that section. A document written before this change may rewrite some of its other sections once, on its first regenerate.
