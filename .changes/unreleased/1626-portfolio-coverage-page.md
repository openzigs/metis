- A new **Portfolio coverage** page (Projects › Portfolio coverage) lets a business
  analyst type a requirement, or start from an approved requirement or a product page,
  and check it against one product or every product they can access. The answer comes
  first in plain words: "Covered by Billing (acme/payments)", "Partly covered by …", or
  "Nothing in your portfolio covers this". A table then lists every product in scope as
  covers, partly covers, does not cover, not checked (with the reason) or restricted.
  A covering or partial product opens to its citations, each named by repository, file
  and line. Progress shows while the check runs, and reloading the page picks the same
  check up again.
