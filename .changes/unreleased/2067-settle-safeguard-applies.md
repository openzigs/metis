- When you answer a company-policy clarifying question by saying a safeguard is already in place
  ("Yes, the supplier passed its review"), METIS now keeps the rule as applying, so the finding can
  become **Compliant**. It used to read such an answer as the rule not applying at all, and the
  finding disappeared. A rule is ruled out only when your answer says what it governs is absent.
