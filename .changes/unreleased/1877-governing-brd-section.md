- A Business Requirements document generated from requirements now has a
  **Governing rules** section listing each governing finding of the analysis:
  status, control id, policy revision, the clause checked, and your accept or
  reject decision (rejected findings are marked, not dropped). It never says
  "compliant" with a framework. Behind `GOVERNING_DOCS_ENABLED`, off by default.
