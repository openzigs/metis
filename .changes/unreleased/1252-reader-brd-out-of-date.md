- A reader-layout BRD without automatic updates says **Out of date** when the
  approved requirements it lists were approved or changed since its latest
  version, with a **Regenerate** button.
- Saving an analysis run's requirements, a run finishing, an import that creates
  requirements and a discussion promoted to a requirement now ask automatic
  updates to check reader BRDs. A generation that published before an approval
  made during it was picked up now checks again on its own.
