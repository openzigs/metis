---
issue: 1237
section: Fixed
---

- Editing a requirement's acceptance criteria now removes the "Check the
  acceptance criteria" note cleanly from text saved with Windows line endings,
  and from a note whose hidden marker was broken across lines, keeping any text
  you typed on the same lines.
- The notices about answers that were not written now name the routes that
  actually write them again: submitting the next answers while questions are
  open, or, once clarification is complete, reopening a rejected requirement on
  the Approvals panel and approving it. They no longer ask you to resubmit
  answers or restore hidden markers, which you cannot do.
