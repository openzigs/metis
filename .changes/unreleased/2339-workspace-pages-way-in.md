- The workspace menu in the header has **Open workspace**. It opens the workspace's own pages, so its **Traceability** and **Governing documents** are each one click away instead of reachable only by typing their address.
- An analysis's **Governing rules** panel links to the workspace's Governing documents, so an analyst told that no rules were checked can go and add a document.
- Every workspace page names the workspace and has a **Back to** link to the project you came from.
- Nothing about who may do what changes: a member who is not a workspace admin still sees the governing documents read-only, and with governing documents turned off no link to them is shown.
