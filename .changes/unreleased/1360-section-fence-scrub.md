---
issue: 1360
section: Security
---

- Untrusted text can no longer close or forge the `=== … ===` fences of the
  absence check, the support panel, the faithfulness judge, the RAGAS relevancy
  judge and the docs-gen mined-rule inventories, exactly or through a Unicode
  look-alike. Clean text reaches the model unchanged.
- An impact requirement title can no longer forge an extra item line in the run
  summary, or close the item list with a short `<<END ITEMS>>` terminator.
