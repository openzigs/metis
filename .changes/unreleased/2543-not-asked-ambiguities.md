- The clarifying-questions note under a round now also counts the open
  ambiguities the round wrote no question for. A round writes questions for at
  most 24 open ambiguities, and the note ("N more questions waiting") counted
  only the questions written and not asked, so on the last round it understated
  what goes unasked and disagreed with the "remaining" counter. It now reads,
  for example, "16 more questions of lower impact are waiting for the next
  round. 220 more open ambiguities have no question yet and are left for later
  rounds."
