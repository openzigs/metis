- A governing finding marked **Needs BA input** on a rule that applies now gets
  its question in the clarifying questions. Before, only rules whose reach was
  still uncertain were asked about, so the finding's "Answer the clarifying
  question" link opened the Questions tab with nothing there to answer. The
  question is asked in plain business words, never naming the rule, and the
  link now goes straight to it. Your answer is kept with its question, and the
  findings for that requirement are checked again with it.
