issue: 2546
section: Security
---

- Members a document's access list leaves out can no longer start a portfolio check or an impact
  analysis from it, and those that someone else started show them none of its text. The analyses
  list no longer sends a run's raw metadata, and a discussion names a requirement drawn from it as
  "Restricted".
- Those members see the document as "Restricted", without its name. They can't tick it for an
  analysis, and it isn't counted as used. A change request started from it doesn't name it to
  them, in its sources or in a title made from its name.
