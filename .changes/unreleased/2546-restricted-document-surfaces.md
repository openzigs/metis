section: Security

- Members a document's access list leaves out can no longer start a portfolio check or an impact
  analysis from it, and those someone else started show them none of its text. The analyses list
  no longer sends a run's raw metadata, and a discussion names a requirement from it "Restricted".
- They see the document as "Restricted" on the Documents page and in quarantine, without its name
  or who may read it, and cannot tick it. A change request made from it is not named after it to
  them anywhere: its page, lists, Requirements, Reviews, chat, or a check or impact analysis.
