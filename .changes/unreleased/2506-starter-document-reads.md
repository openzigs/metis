issue: 2506
section: Security
---

- An analysis you start now reads documents with your own access too, including the restricted ones
  you can see. Anyone who can't see one of them sees what drew on it as "Restricted", and none of
  what rests on the run as a whole: its reviewed list, web research, governing rules and findings,
  escalation and clarifying questions (they also can't start or answer a clarification round).
  The notice counts the restricted files and documents read, never which. Answers given on such a
  run are not added to the project's searchable knowledge, and generated documents skip its web
  research. Scheduled analyses still use only what everyone on the project can see.
