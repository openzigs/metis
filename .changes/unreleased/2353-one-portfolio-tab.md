section: Changed
---

- The Projects hub has four tabs: **All projects**, **Portfolio**, **Documents** and **Impact
  analyses**. **Portfolio** holds what **Products** and **Portfolio coverage** showed:
  **Coverage**, where you check a requirement across your products, and **Products**, where you
  list, open and create them. `/products` opens the Products section, `/portfolio` the coverage
  check, and a product's own page keeps its address.
- The **Repositories** and **Databases** tabs are gone. A project's repositories and databases are
  on its **Knowledge** tab, as before. `/repositories` and `/databases` open the project list, or a
  project's Knowledge tab when the link names one (`?projectId=`). The one thing only those tabs
  gave, a single list of every connection across all projects, is not kept: open each project
  instead.
