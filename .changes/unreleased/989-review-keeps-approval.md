section: Fixed
---

- Requesting a review no longer resets approved requirements to draft. An approved requirement
  stays approved while it is under review and changes only if the review is rejected. Withdrawing
  the review, or closing it before a decision, puts every requirement back to the status it had
  before. The Request review form says how many of the requirements are already approved, and the
  Requirements hub's counts update as soon as the review is requested.
