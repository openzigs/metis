- Each requirement card now lists up to five similar requirements in the same
  project, labelled "Likely duplicate", "Overlaps" or "Related", with their
  status and how many of their code links are fresh or stale. A likely
  duplicate shows a warning with **Mark as duplicate** and **Not a duplicate**;
  "Not a duplicate" is remembered for that pair after a reload. While you type
  new requirements to evaluate, the same list appears for your text.
