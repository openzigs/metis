section: Added

- You can now restrict who can read a document from the UI: choose **Access** on the document's
  row on the Documents page, or in the Quarantine list, and name the roles or users who may read
  it. A restricted document shows a **Restricted** badge: its readers see who else can read it;
  a member it leaves out sees only "Restricted" and cannot pick it, approve, reject or re-tag it,
  or change its access list. Refused attempts are audited.
