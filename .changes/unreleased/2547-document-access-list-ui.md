section: Added

- You can now restrict who can read a document from the UI: choose **Access** on the document's
  row on the Documents page, or in the Quarantine list before approving it, and name the roles or
  users who may read it. A restricted document shows a **Restricted** badge saying who can read
  it, on the Documents page, in the Quarantine list and in the new change request and impact
  analysis document pickers. A member who is not on the list sees that they cannot read it, and
  cannot pick it for a change request, start a portfolio check from it, or change its access list;
  the editor warns you before you save a list that leaves you off.
