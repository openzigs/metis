- On the Requirements list, **Approve** on a requirement you wrote or edited is disabled with the
  reason before you click it, when the project does not allow self-approval.
- The **Approval gate** card, with **Allow self-approval**, is now also in the project's
  **⚙ Project settings**, and the approval note and refusal message point there.
