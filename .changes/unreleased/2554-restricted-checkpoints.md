---
issue: 2554
section: Security
---

- A member a document's access list leaves out can no longer approve, reject or reopen a
  checkpoint resting on it, approve all or leave out the added while one is restricted, or
  regenerate, cancel or resume a change request that read it, nor ask for a review of a
  requirement drawn from it; the buttons are hidden and refused attempts are audited. Starting a
  change request from such a document is refused before anything is spent, and an impact
  analysis of a restricted requirement now answers "Requirement not found".
