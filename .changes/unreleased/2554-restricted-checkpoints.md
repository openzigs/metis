---
issue: 2554
section: Security
---

- A member a document's access list leaves out can no longer approve, reject or reopen a
  checkpoint resting on it, approve all or leave out the added while one is restricted, or
  regenerate, cancel or resume a change request that read it, nor ask for, be named reviewer
  of, or decide a review of a requirement drawn from it; the buttons are hidden and refused
  attempts are audited. Starting a change request from such a document is refused before
  anything is spent, and an impact analysis of a restricted, unknown or unreachable
  requirement answers the same "Requirement not found".
