---
issue: 2132
section: Fixed
---

- A settlement's reason no longer turns a control id such as SOC 2 "A1.2" or "Annex A1" into
  "the analyst's answer". A reason that cites several answers now says "the analyst's answers".
- A policy citation that contains an abbreviation such as "U.S." or "Art. 32" now quotes the
  whole sentence instead of starting partway through it.
- A clarifying question that called something just "Requirement" now names the requirement
  by its title instead of leaving it unnamed, and a requirement listed inside brackets keeps
  both brackets.
