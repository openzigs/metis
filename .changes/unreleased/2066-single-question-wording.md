- A company-policy clarifying question that is not combined with another is now
  asked in its original words. Before, when a requirement had several such
  questions, the step that combines duplicates could reword one it left on its
  own, and the reworded question could be read the other way: "Yes, we do."
  then marked the rule as not applying instead of satisfied.
