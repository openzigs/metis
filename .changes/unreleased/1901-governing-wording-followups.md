- The governing wording guard reads better and flags less. "It has passed SOC 2" now becomes
  "It has been checked against SOC 2" instead of "It has was checked against SOC 2". "The value
  passes the policy limit" is no longer treated as a compliance claim, and neither is
  "hasn't passed SOC 2". The summary sentence also keeps "compliant" out when the word is
  joined to another, as in a document titled "Noncompliant Rules".
