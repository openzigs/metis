section: Changed
---

- The **Requirements** tab lists every requirement across change requests, with its change
  request, status and review; filter, search and page it, and approve, edit or request review
  from a row. It states the approval ladder: checkpoint, direct approval, formal review.
- The run's **Approvals** tab is now **Checkpoints**. An approved card no longer offers Approve or
  Reject. A rejected review says why and offers **Revise and resubmit**. The reviewer search says
  when you are on your own, and what happens then.
