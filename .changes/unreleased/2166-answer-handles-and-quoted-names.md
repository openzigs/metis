section: Fixed
---

- A governing-rule reason such as "Per Annex A3, A1 and A2 agree." now reads "Per Annex A3, the
  analyst's answers agree." instead of showing the raw answer labels. A list of annex parts in
  order ("Annex A1, A2 and A3") is still left as written.
- A clarifying question that names a requirement by a quoted title ending in "!" or "?" (the
  "Login!" requirement) no longer has the title added a second time.
