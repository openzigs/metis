- The governing wording guard catches claims it had started to miss and reads better on the rest.
  "The feature passes the policy check" is a claim again and becomes "The feature was checked
  against the policy". "It has only partly complied with GDPR" becomes "It has been checked against
  GDPR" instead of "It has only partly was checked against GDPR", and "The vendor's passed SOC 2
  report" becomes "The vendor's SOC 2-reviewed report". A negation is left alone even with an
  adverb in between ("It hasn't successfully passed SOC 2", "not currently SOC 2 compliant"), and
  "passes the policy-mandated limit" or "passes the policy's limit" is no longer read as a claim.
