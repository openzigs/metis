---
issue: 941
section: Security
---

- Accepting a workspace invitation now requires signing in as the invited email address. Before,
  anyone holding the invite link could add the invited account to the workspace without signing
  in. A signed-out visitor who clicks **Accept invitation** is sent to sign in and then back to
  the invitation; an account with a different email address is refused.
- Workspace owners and admins can now invite members from **Settings → Workspaces**. Enter an
  email address and a role to get an invite link to share; no invitation email is sent yet.
