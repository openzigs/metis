---
issue: 679
section: Security
---

- Live "who is viewing" avatars on discussions and Spec Kit artifacts now show a
  viewer list only to people who can open that discussion or project. Before, any
  signed-in user could see who was viewing any artifact, in any project or
  workspace, by naming its id. A viewer refused on re-joining, for example after
  losing access, is now also removed from the list.
