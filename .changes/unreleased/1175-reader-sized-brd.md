- `DOCS_GEN_BRD_LAYOUT=reader` (new, **off by default**): a full-scope Business Requirements
  document opens with a main document of about 12–18 pages for the people who sign it off
  (Executive Summary, Scope, Core Business Capabilities, Key Business Rules, and Requirements
  with acceptance criteria), followed by an appendix with the module-by-module detail. The
  main part is held to about 60,000 characters; anything it leaves out is listed. Unset, a
  BRD is generated exactly as before.
- The walkthrough scores the BRD on a sign-off rubric as well as the answer key.
