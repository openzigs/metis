# User Story: Price Watchlist

> Original synthetic document authored for the METIS Domain Eval golden corpus (CC0-1.0).

## Overview

As a buyer, I want a watchlist so I am told when prices drop.

## Requirements

- A buyer must be able to add a product to a personal watchlist.
- The system must alert a buyer when a watched product's price drops.
- A buyer may optionally share a watchlist with a friend.
