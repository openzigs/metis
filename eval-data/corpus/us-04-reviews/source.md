# User Story: Product Reviews

> Original synthetic document authored for the METIS Domain Eval golden corpus (CC0-1.0).

## Overview

As a customer, I want to review products so others can decide.

## Requirements

- A customer must be able to submit a star rating and a written review.
- A customer should be able to edit a review they previously posted.
- The service must fix the defect that posts duplicate reviews when a submission is retried.
