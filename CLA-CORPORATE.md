# METIS Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement (v1.0)

This agreement is for **employers**, not for individual contributors. If you are
contributing on your own account, sign the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md) instead.

**Why this exists.** In most jurisdictions and most employment contracts, work an
employee produces in the course of their employment belongs to their employer, not to
them. An individual CLA signed by such a contributor grants rights they may not
actually hold — clause 5 of the ICLA is where that surfaces, and it is a
representation the contributor can be personally wrong about in good faith. This
Corporate CLA is how the employer grants those rights directly, so nobody has to
guess.

Most people who contribute to a project of this kind do so with an employer in the
background. That is why this document exists rather than being deferred.

the outstanding shares, or (iii) beneficial ownership of such entity.

"Contribution" shall mean the code, documentation or other original works of
authorship expressly identified in Schedule B, as well as any original work of
authorship, including any modifications or additions to an existing work, that is
intentionally submitted by You to the Company for inclusion in, or documentation of,
METIS (the "Work"). For the purposes of this definition, "submitted" means any form of
electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to the Company or its
representatives, including but not limited to communication on source-code control
systems, pull requests and issue-tracking systems that are managed by, or on behalf
of, the Company for the purpose of discussing and improving the Work, but excluding
communication that is conspicuously marked or otherwise designated in writing by You
as "Not a Contribution."

## 4. Recipients receive their rights from METIS's outbound licence, not from You

Nothing in this Agreement grants any licence directly from You to any recipient of
METIS. Recipients receive their rights under the licence the Company distributes METIS
under — `AGPL-3.0-only` today. The reasoning is the same as in clause 4 of the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md#4-recipients-receive-their-rights-from-metiss-outbound-licence-not-from-you):
a direct, condition-free grant to every downstream recipient would let them bypass the
copyleft entirely.

## 9. Notification

It is Your responsibility to notify the Company when any change is required to the
list of designated employees authorised to submit Contributions on behalf of the
Corporation, or to the Corporation's point of contact.

## How to execute this Agreement

The bot on pull requests records individual signatures only; a corporate agreement is
a document between two legal entities and is executed out of band.

1. Complete the details below.
2. Send the completed document to the address published on the project's contact page,
   which is the address of record for legal correspondence.
3. Have your designated employees sign the [Individual CLA](CLA.md) as well, through
   the bot on their first pull request. The two are complementary: the Corporate CLA
   settles what the Corporation holds, the Individual CLA settles what the individual
   holds.
